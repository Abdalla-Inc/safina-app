# Habit engine research — 25 September 2026

## Scope and evidence standard
This is a desk review of original papers, reviews and public product documentation, not a trial in Safinat Al-Nur. A = replicated randomized/review evidence in another behavior; B = substantial observational or mixed intervention evidence; C = theory/limited association; D = untested transfer to devotional reading. The 30-credit threshold is a founder decision; visual milestones and cue frequencies remain design hypotheses. Product documentation verifies a mechanic, not its effect. Public documentation was inspected on 2026-09-25; installed binary versions, complete onboarding and private re-entry flows were not verified.

| Claim and source | Grade, limitation | Engine implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Lally et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674), 96 participants repeating eating/drinking/activity in context; automaticity varied widely and one missed opportunity did not materially disrupt formation. [2024 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11641623/) reports heterogeneous timelines. | B for context repetition; D for Qur’an or high reading loads. No universal 21/66-day promise. | Let members attach reading to a chosen existing cue; avoid a catastrophic missed-day reset. |
| [Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006](https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/publications/implementation-intentions-and-goal-achievement-meta-analysis-effects-and-processes), meta-analysis of if/then plans. | A generally, D transfer; effect varies with behavior and commitment. | Optional “After [routine], at [place], I’ll read [portion]”; fallback plan. |
| [Michie et al. 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19916637/), meta-regression of self-monitoring plus self-regulation feedback in diet/activity; [later synthesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7429262/) notes mixed incremental effects. | B/C; component associations do not isolate this app’s effect. | Short truthful log and factual personal feedback; test reading outcomes. |
| [Deci & Ryan 2000](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15327965PLI1104_01) autonomy/competence/relatedness theory; [health RCT review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31983293/) finds heterogeneous effects. | B for health, D devotional transfer. | User-selected level, controllable cues, private progress, supportive coach. |
| [Epton et al. 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29189034/) goal-setting RCT meta-analysis, small average benefit. | A general, D transfer; public/difficult-goal moderators do not justify worship comparisons. | Offer concrete personal plan and revision, never public volume goals. |
| [Kwasnicka et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4975085/) maintenance theories: motives, self-regulation, context and resources. | C theory synthesis, not feature trial. | Weekly optional reflection about barriers and realistic commitment. |
| [Deci et al. 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10589297/) meta-analysis: some expected tangible contingent rewards undermine intrinsic motivation; [health gamification review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28536062/) finds limited efficacy evidence. | B general, D for symbolic ship. | Ship is predictable informational acknowledgement; no random loot, loss, purchase or scarcity. |
| [2025 social-comparison meta-analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02209-2) finds small short-term behavior effects across domains. | B general, D religious appropriateness. | Opt-in encouragement without rankings or exact amount. |
| [Bell et al. 2023 notification trial](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2023/1/e38342) improved short-term app openings, without significant disengagement difference. | B for app opens, no evidence for reading. | Never optimize notification taps; measure reading and burden. |
| [Hagerman et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10543633/) and [Thøgersen-Ntoumani et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8451927/) suggest gentler lapse reactions in dietary contexts, mixed longer-term outcomes. | C/D observational transfer. | Compassionate re-entry is an ethical choice and testable hypothesis, not guaranteed retention. |

## Domain reference
[Quran Foundation content snapshot](https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/content_apis_versioned/4.0.0/resources-snapshot/) specifies 114 chapters, 6,236 verses and 30 juz for its Uthmani mapping; [Juz API](https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/content_apis_versioned/4.0.0/list-juzs/) exposes chapter/verse mapping. Cross-check against [Al Quran Cloud Juz index](https://alquran.cloud/juzs) and [Surah index](https://alquran.cloud/surahs). Pin a licensed snapshot and its verse numbering before production; documentation alone is not an imported, validated dataset. [Quran Foundation page layout guide](https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/fonts/page-layout/) explains Mushaf-specific pages. No religious rule is inferred from metadata.

## Risks and countermeasures
| Failure | Countermeasure and test |
|---|---|
| Shame, avoidance after lapse | No decay or red failure state; interview returning members; monitor guilt reports. |
| Fictional logging to preserve progress | Partial credit and no streak cliff; allow corrections; never infer truth from taps or timers. |
| Compulsive checks or reward dependence | Deterministic earned states only, one acknowledgement, no variable drops or daily mystery. |
| Overload at high commitments | Neutral capacity check and change effective tomorrow; monitor abandoned plans by level. |
| Notification pressure | Off by default, one opted-in chosen cue, silence after log/pause, weekly frequency cap; compare reading and opt-outs. |
| Spiritual competition or leakage | Reading history private; optional moderated encouragement without quantities, identities/retention consent and deletion controls. |

## Research still needed
Arabic-language and accessibility usability sessions with low/high levels, returning readers, older members, intermittent routines; assess right-to-left labels, numerals, screen-reader pronunciations, large text, simple one-handed logging, offline edits and no mandatory timer. These are requirements/hypotheses, not evaluated outcomes. Coach interviews and current program documents are required for official levels and cadence.

## Founder application note — added after voice review
The founder cited [Forest](https://www.forestapp.cc/) as the closest growth metaphor and requested a private calendar showing commitment category color plus actual reading status, with separate khatma marks. This is founder direction, not evidence that visible growth or streaks cause more Qur’an reading. The designer should examine Forest hands-on before later design work; this desk audit only read public documentation. The 30-credit ship, weekly/monthly cadence and Nuh-inspired verse framing came from founder discussion and are documented in [rules](PROGRAM_RULES_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), not derived from these papers. The wording, context and use of any Qur’anic verse need a separate founder check; no research source here validates a religious protection promise.
