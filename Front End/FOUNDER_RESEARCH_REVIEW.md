## Four decisions that materially affect feasibility

**1. Ad-free library delivery.** Standard [YouTube embeds can show ads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/132596?hl=en); [privacy-enhanced embedding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en) does not remove that risk. Recommended resolution: authorized coach source files with an approved ad-free host/player. If external YouTube links remain, decide how to describe that boundary honestly. No provider restrictions should be bypassed.

**2. A compatible Qur'an source bundle.** [Tanzil](https://tanzil.net/download/) provides versioned text with [verbatim/attribution conditions](https://tanzil.net/docs/Text_License); its [metadata](https://tanzil.net/docs/Quran_Metadata) needs edition and permission checks. [Quran Foundation terms](https://api-docs.quran.com/legal/developer-terms/) provide another route with content, caching and source-specific conditions. The content/reference owners must approve text, metadata, page mapping, fonts and exact versions together. Audio requires separate permission. No provider was selected or scripture synthesized.

**3. Truthful recording semantics.** A completed row gesture is a deliberate self-report, not proof or a frontend reward calculation. A reader suggestion is not confirmed reading. A resized screen is not an edition page. Same-day continuation, partial pages, overlaps and real repetitions need approved engine examples. Show saved-local and evaluation-pending separately.

**4. Publication as a separate action.** A circle member starts private. Consent names audience, activity, detail and review/automatic mode. Revocation must cover queued events; corrections/deletions need a linked-post policy. Saving a private log must never itself authorize a group update or disclose exact passages, level or dhikr count.

## Arabic, accessibility and member evidence

Keep RTL reading order and mixed-direction references correct; test long Arabic labels, punctuation, diacritics and numerals at 200% text. The partial gesture needs a tap/stepper equivalent, not haptics or color alone. [W3C guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html) supports that requirement. Test 360×800 and 430×932 logical viewports plus real phones, Arabic VoiceOver/TalkBack, reduced motion, offline retries and correction recovery. These are planned checks, not verified compliance.

Draft copy examples are `ابدأ القراءة` (Start reading), `تسجيل مقترح من القارئ — راجعه قبل اعتماده` (Suggested reader entry — review before confirming), and `لم تتم مشاركة هذا التسجيل` (This entry has not been shared). A fluent Arabic reviewer must validate interface language; the coach reviews religious wording. No draft claims intention, sincerity, divine reward or spiritual worth.

Recruit 10–12 consenting adults with overlapping lower/higher commitments, long absence, older/less technical members, different Arabic fluency and devices, free/paid members and two circle administrators; include assistive-technology users or record the gap. Interview present habits first. After selection, test partial/undo, false suggestion correction, physical overlap, offline entry, return, lesson-note retrieval, written-answer recovery, istighfar search, joining without sharing and revocation. No interviews were fabricated or recruitment messages sent.
